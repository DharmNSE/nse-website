Privacy Notice

How we handle personal information you give us through this website.

Who we are

Nuclear Safety Engineering Limited ("NSE", "we") is the data controller for personal data collected through this website. We are registered in England and Wales (company no. 08467393), with our registered office at 2 Wheeleys Road, Edgbaston, Birmingham, B15 2LD. Contact: info@nuclearsafetyengineering.com.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry we collect the details you provide: your name, email address, organisation, sector and message. We do not use cookies or analytics on this website.

Why we use it

We use your details only to respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, to discuss or provide our services. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries, or steps taken at your request before entering into a contract.

Who we share it with

Enquiries submitted through the form are delivered to us by our form-processing provider (Formspree), which acts as our processor. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts; these providers may process your IP address to deliver the site. We do not sell your data or share it for marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry correspondence for as long as needed to deal with it and for up to two years afterwards, unless it leads to a contract, in which case it is kept in line with our contract records.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or erase your personal data, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). To exercise your rights, email us at the address above.
