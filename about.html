Who we are

Built on expertise. Defined by rigour.

Nuclear Safety Engineering was established in 2013 to give clients direct access to senior specialists with real nuclear and defence experience.

Our story

Founded on a principle

NSE was founded by practitioners who had spent their careers on nuclear and defence programmes, and who understood that the difference between a good consultant and a great one is direct experience.

As an SME, we have stayed close to those roots. Our consultants are senior practitioners, and the specialist you meet is the specialist who does the work.

Over more than a decade we have built a reputation for technical rigour, independent judgement and dependable delivery in demanding regulated environments.

Our heritage

In nuclear and defence, failure is measured in safety, security and public trust, not just cost. That heritage shapes everything we do: rigour, governance and independent scrutiny are how NSE operates on every project.
