Ethics Policy

Nuclear Safety Engineering Limited · Approved by the Board

Nuclear Safety Engineering Limited (NSE) is committed to conducting all business activities with integrity, transparency and respect. This policy sets out the ethical standards expected of all NSE personnel and the principles that govern our professional conduct.

Our commitment

NSE operates with honesty and integrity in all dealings. We are committed to treating clients, colleagues, suppliers and regulators with fairness and respect, and to maintaining the trust that underpins our professional relationships. We do not engage in, condone or facilitate conduct that is dishonest, misleading or harmful to others.

Conflicts of interest

All NSE personnel are required to identify and disclose any actual or potential conflict of interest that could compromise, or be perceived to compromise, their professional judgement. Where a conflict is identified, it must be declared to management promptly. NSE will take appropriate steps to manage or mitigate the conflict, which may include recusal from the relevant engagement.

Confidentiality

NSE personnel handle client information, project data and commercially sensitive material with strict confidentiality. Information obtained in the course of an engagement is used solely for the purposes of that engagement and is not disclosed to third parties without appropriate authorisation. Confidentiality obligations continue after the conclusion of an engagement.

Professional conduct

NSE personnel are expected to maintain the highest standards of professional conduct at all times: in client interactions, in the quality of technical work, and in the way they represent the company. We do not make claims we cannot substantiate, and we provide honest, evidence-based advice even when it is not what a client wants to hear.

Reporting concerns

Any NSE personnel who becomes aware of conduct that may breach this policy is encouraged to raise the matter promptly. NSE maintains a confidential reporting mechanism and will not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith. Reports may be made directly to senior management.

Policy review

This policy is reviewed annually and updated as necessary to reflect changes in legislation, regulatory guidance and best practice. All NSE personnel are required to familiarise themselves with the current version of this policy and to complete any associated training.

Anti-Corruption Policy

Nuclear Safety Engineering Limited · Approved by the Board

NSE has a zero-tolerance approach to bribery and corruption in all its forms. This policy sets out our obligations under the Bribery Act 2010 and the standards we apply to all business activities, whether conducted directly or through third parties.

Zero tolerance

NSE will not offer, pay, request or accept bribes or improper payments of any kind, whether in cash, gifts, hospitality or any other form of benefit, to or from any person or organisation, public or private, in any jurisdiction. This prohibition applies to all NSE personnel, contractors and any third parties acting on NSE's behalf.

Scope and application

This policy applies to all NSE personnel, including directors, employees, contractors and consultants, and to all business activities conducted by or on behalf of NSE, regardless of geography. It covers direct conduct and conduct facilitated through agents, intermediaries, joint venture partners or any other third party.

Gifts and hospitality

NSE personnel may give or receive modest, reasonable gifts and hospitality where this is consistent with normal business practice, transparent and proportionate. Gifts or hospitality must never be offered or accepted with the intention, or reasonable appearance, of influencing a business decision. Any gift or hospitality above a nominal value must be declared and recorded. Cash gifts are never acceptable.

Facilitation payments

NSE strictly prohibits facilitation payments: unofficial payments made to government officials or others to secure or expedite routine actions to which NSE or its clients are legitimately entitled. Such payments are illegal under the Bribery Act 2010 and are prohibited regardless of local custom or practice.

Due diligence

Before engaging agents, intermediaries or other third parties to act on NSE's behalf, appropriate due diligence will be conducted to assess the risk of bribery or corruption. NSE will not engage third parties who are unwilling to commit to equivalent anti-corruption standards, and will include appropriate contractual provisions in all relevant agreements.

Record keeping

NSE maintains accurate and transparent records of all financial transactions, gifts, hospitality and payments made in the course of business. These records are available for review by management and, where required, by regulatory or law enforcement authorities.

Reporting and whistleblowing

Any NSE personnel who suspects that a bribe has been offered, paid, requested or received, or that this policy has been breached in any other way, must report the matter to senior management immediately. NSE will investigate all reports promptly and will not retaliate against anyone who raises a concern in good faith. Where appropriate, matters will be referred to the relevant authorities.

Consequences of breach

Breach of this policy is a serious matter and may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contract. Individuals who engage in bribery or corruption may also face personal criminal liability under the Bribery Act 2010. NSE will cooperate fully with any investigation by law enforcement or regulatory authorities.

These policies are reviewed annually by NSE's senior management and communicated to all personnel at induction and through ongoing training. If you have any questions or wish to raise a concern, please contact us.
