DISCIPLINE 01

Design

Engineered from first principles

From early concept through to detailed design and construction support, we apply structured engineering methods to deliver solutions that are safe, buildable and compliant with demanding regulatory frameworks.

We apply the same disciplined approach to every project, from defence nuclear facilities to complex industrial plant.

What we deliver

  • Concept and feasibility studies
  • Detailed engineering design
  • Design safety justification
  • Technical specifications and standards compliance
  • Independent design review
  • Construction and commissioning support
DISCIPLINE 02

Safety

Safety as a foundation, not a function

We provide independent safety assessment, hazard analysis and regulatory support across the full project lifecycle.

Our safety professionals bring direct experience of safety assessment and regulatory engagement on UK defence nuclear programmes.

What we deliver

  • Nuclear safety case development and review
  • Hazard identification and risk assessment (HAZID/HAZOP)
  • Design basis and fault analysis
  • Safety management system development
  • Independent nuclear safety assessment
  • Regulatory engagement support
